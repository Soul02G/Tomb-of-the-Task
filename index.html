About the Game

What is Tomb of the Task?

A faithful recreation of the classic arcade game Tomb of the Mask — an increasingly difficult labyrinth experience built by five developers. The gameplay revolves around a dash mechanic that sends your character flying from wall to wall. Collect coins and stars, survive spike traps, and defeat enemies to reach the finish line.

Instructions

How to Play

Navigate the labyrinth and reach the finish line of each level. You move by dashing in a straight line until you hit a wall. Beware of anything that causes your character to perish.

WASD / Arrow Keys

Dash — move from wall to wall

M

Open settings

Enter

Change settings

Select level

Features

What's Inside

  • Labyrinths

    Hand-crafted levels with increasing difficulty, accessible via a level selector interface.

  • Dash Mechanics

    Fluid wall-to-wall movement with camera tracking strictly centered on the character at all times.

  • Rebound Tiles

    Special squares that redirect the player toward the direction they are facing upon contact.

  • Red & Blue Tiles

    Alternating tiles that turn solid or passable each time the player moves.

  • Spikes & Traps

    Static spikes and deploying spike tiles that trigger after a brief delay — instant death on contact.

  • Economy

    Coins and stars scattered throughout levels, collected into a persistent counter.

  • Enemies

    Bats, Archers, and Monkeys — each with unique movement patterns and projectile attacks.

  • Full UI System

    Configuration menu with sliders, pause menu, game data saving, completion screen, and credits.

Developer Options

Debug Codes

LCTRL + 1 + P — Immortality

The character cannot die under any circumstance.

LCTRL + 2 + P — Hitbox View

Replaces all sprites with their hitbox outlines.

LCTRL + 3 + P — Maxed Out

Unlocks all levels with 3 stars and sets coins to 999. Must be inside a level to activate.
